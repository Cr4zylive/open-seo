CBM calculator for shipping

Cubic meters only. Not UPS dimensional weight in inches, and not a freight quote.

How CBM is calculated

Convert each side to meters, then length × width × height × quantity.

Example: 100 × 50 × 40 cm × 2 cartons = 1.00 × 0.50 × 0.40 × 2 = 0.40 m³.

What this page is not

It does not apply air chargeable weight (÷5000 / ÷6000) or UPS Daily 139 / Retail 166. Those are different formulas on different sites.
