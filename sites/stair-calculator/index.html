Stair calculator

Straight run only. Enter the floor-to-floor rise. The page picks a whole number of risers near your target height.

How it is calculated

  1. Risers = round(total rise ÷ target riser), at least 2.
  2. Actual riser = total rise ÷ risers.
  3. Treads on a straight run = risers − 1.
  4. Total run = treads × tread depth. Stringer = √(rise² + run²).

Example: 105 in rise, 7.5 in target, 10 in treads → 14 risers of 7.5 in, 13 treads, 130 in run.

IRC R311.7 often caps residential risers at 7.75 in and treads at a 10 in minimum. Local code wins.

What this page is not

It is not L-shaped stairs, landings, headroom, or a permit drawing. Not building-code advice.
