UPS dimensional weight calculator

One carton. UPS Daily or Retail rates. The billable weight is whichever is higher: the scale weight, or length × width × height ÷ the divisor.

How UPS calculates it

  1. Measure each side at the longest point and round to the nearest whole inch.
  2. Multiply: length × width × height for cubic inches.
  3. Divide by the DIM factor: 139 for Daily rates, 166 for Retail rates.
  4. If the result is not a whole number, go up to the next whole pound.
  5. UPS charges the greater of that dimensional weight and the actual scale weight.

Worked example on this page: 16 × 12 × 12 in = 2,304 in³. Daily: 2,304 ÷ 139 ≈ 16.58 → 17 lb. Retail: 2,304 ÷ 166 ≈ 13.88 → 14 lb. A 10 lb scale weight still bills at 17 lb on Daily rates.

Source: UPS Shipping Dimensions and Weight. Account contracts can use a different divisor. This page is not a UPS product and does not quote a price.

What this page is not

It is not FedEx, DHL, USPS, or air-freight chargeable weight. Those carriers publish other divisors. It is not legal or shipping advice.
