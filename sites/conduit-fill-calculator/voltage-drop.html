Voltage drop calculator

Single-phase uses 2KIL / CM. Three-phase uses √3 KIL / CM. K is 12.9 for copper and 21.2 for aluminum.

Worked example

120 V, 20 A, 100 ft, #12 copper, single-phase: 2 × 12.9 × 20 × 100 ÷ 6530 ≈ 7.90 V (6.6%). NEC informational notes often mention 3% on a branch circuit — this run would be long for #12.

What this page is not

It ignores temperature, power factor, and conduit material. It is not a load calculation and not engineering advice.
