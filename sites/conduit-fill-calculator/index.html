NEC conduit fill calculator

One raceway. THHN/THWN conductors. Fill limits are 53% / 31% / 40% from NEC Chapter 9 Table 1.

How fill is calculated

  1. Look up each THHN area in Chapter 9 Table 5 and add them up.
  2. Look up the raceway internal area in Table 4.
  3. Allowed fill is 53% (1 wire), 31% (2 wires), or 40% (3 or more).

Worked example: 9 × 12 AWG THHN = 0.1197 in². ½″ EMT is 0.304 in². 40% = 0.1216 in². Nine conductors pass; ten (0.1330 in²) fail.

Tables follow NEC Chapter 9. This page is not the code book and is not a substitute for the edition your jurisdiction adopted.

What this page is not

It does not model nipples (24 in or less), compact conductors, XHHW areas, derating, or boxes. It is not engineering advice.
