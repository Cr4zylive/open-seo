Wire size calculator

Finds the smallest THHN-sized conductor (14 AWG–4/0) that keeps voltage drop at or under your limit. Ampacity and derating are not applied.

Worked example

120 V, 20 A, 100 ft, copper, 3% cap: #10 is about 4.1% (too high). #8 is about 2.6% and is the first size that passes.

What this page is not

It does not apply NEC 310.16 ampacity, bundling, or temperature correction. A #8 that passes drop can still be illegal for the breaker. Not engineering advice.
